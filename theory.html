The Physics Behind It

Understanding how objects move through the air.

📐

The Concept

Projectile motion is the curved path an object follows when thrown near the Earth's surface. Think of a football kick or a water fountain.

Once launched, the only force acting on the object is Gravity (and air resistance, if we are being precise). This creates a predictable curved path known as a Parabola.

📜

History

Before the 17th century, the "Impetus Theory" suggested that objects moved in straight lines until they ran out of energy, then fell straight down.

It was Galileo Galilei who changed everything. He realized that horizontal motion and vertical motion are independent. He proved that the path is actually a curve, not a triangle!

∑

Equations

To predict where the ball lands, we split the velocity into X (horizontal) and Y (vertical) components.

Horizontal Distance (x):
Moves at constant speed.

x = v₀ · cos(θ) · t

Vertical Height (y):
Fights against gravity.

y = v₀ · sin(θ) · t - ½ · g · t²
